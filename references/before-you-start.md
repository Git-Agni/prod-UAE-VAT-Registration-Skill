# Before you start: checklist

Gather all of this before opening the form. Missing items are what stall the application halfway.

## Access

- [ ] EmaraTax account, logged in by the user (email + OTP, or UAE PASS).
- [ ] A Taxable Person profile for the company. If the company registered for Corporate Tax, it already exists and most of Steps 1 to 4 prefill.

## Figures

- [ ] Taxable supplies **per month** since incorporation (or the last 12 months), in AED, counted by invoice date. See `thresholds-and-timing.md`.
- [ ] A list of the invoices behind those figures (number, customer, date, currency, amount).
- [ ] Expected taxable supplies in the next 30 days (from invoices about to go out or signed contracts).
- [ ] Optional: taxable expenses per month (only matters if registering voluntarily on expenses).

## Documents (PDF)

- [ ] Trade licence.
- [ ] Memorandum of Association (proof the signatory can act for the company).
- [ ] Emirates ID and passport of the manager / authorised signatory.
- [ ] **Bank account confirmation letter** showing the company name and IBAN (banks call it a bank letter, IBAN certificate or account confirmation; digital banks issue it in the app).
- [ ] 1 to 3 **sample sales invoices**, signed and stamped by the authorised signatory.
- [ ] **Turnover declaration letter** on the FTA template, signed and stamped (`tools/turnover_declaration.py` can draft it).
- [ ] Optional but helpful: client contracts or purchase orders.

## Company details

- [ ] Registered address exactly as on the trade licence.
- [ ] UAE mobile number and email.
- [ ] Bank name, IBAN, account holder name as on the bank letter.
- [ ] Who the manager / CEO is, and whether they are a UAE resident (the user answers this).
- [ ] Whether the company imports or exports goods **or services**, and whether it has a customs registration number.

## A stamp

The FTA wants the declaration and invoices "signed and stamped." The UAE has no central register of company stamps: companies order a rubber stamp from any stamp shop (around AED 100) or use a digital seal of the company name and licence number. Only ever make a seal for the user's own company.
