# Thresholds, timing and the counting rules

Plain-language summary for the agent to explain to the user. The law is Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on VAT and its Executive Regulation; penalties are in Cabinet Decision No. 49 of 2021. Figures as of 2026; check tax.gov.ae if in doubt. This is orientation, not advice.

## The two thresholds

| | Threshold | When it applies | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory | AED 375,000 | Taxable supplies (plus imports) in the last 12 months passed it, **or** will within the next 30 days | Yes. Apply within 30 days. |
| Voluntary | AED 187,500 | Taxable supplies **or** taxable expenses in the last 12 months passed it, or will within the next 30 days | Optional |

- **Zero-rated supplies count.** Services exported to customers outside the UAE are usually taxed at 0%, but they are still taxable supplies, so they count toward both thresholds. "My clients are abroad" does not mean "I do not need to register."
- **Exempt supplies do not count** (some financial services, residential property, and so on).
- **Late registration:** a fixed AED 10,000 administrative penalty if a mandatory registrant applies late.

## Count by invoice date, not cash

The value of a supply is counted at its date of supply, which for services is generally the earliest of the invoice date, the payment date or the completion of the service. In practice:

- An invoice issued on 3 October counts in October, even if it is paid in November.
- A payment received with no invoice counts on the payment date.
- Counting only cash received can understate the 12-month figure. On the portal, that can flip the registration criteria to "Not Applicable" and trigger a "below threshold" warning.

Build a month-by-month table from the company's own records (invoice register or accounting), convert each amount to AED (USD at the 3.6725 peg; other currencies at the rate of the day), and keep the invoice behind every line. That table goes into Step 3 and into the turnover declaration letter.

## The next-30-days field

The form asks for expected taxable supplies in the next 30 days. Enter a realistic figure from invoices or contracts the company is about to issue. Note: on the portal, a strong next-30-days figure does **not** clear the "below threshold" warning on its own. The past months have to carry it, or you register later when they do.

## Exception from registration

A business whose supplies are **only zero-rated** can apply for an exception from mandatory registration. Trade-off: no VAT returns to file, but no reclaim of input VAT on costs. It is a choice for the user (with a tax agent if unsure), not for the agent.

## After approval

- The FTA's stated processing time is 20 business days from a complete application; simple ones are often faster. It may email for more information.
- The VAT TRN and certificate appear in EmaraTax.
- Tax periods are assigned by the FTA (commonly quarterly). Each return is due **28 days after the period ends**, with any VAT payable by the same date.
- From the effective date, tax invoices must show the company's TRN, and VAT on imported services is usually accounted for under the reverse charge.
